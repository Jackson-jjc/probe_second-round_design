| **01 · Ask About a Detail**Choose part of an object image. Say what you notice. Ask AI one question, then say what you think after its answer.<br>**We collect:** the chosen detail, question, actual answer and any change in the visitor’s explanation.<br> | **After an AI answer, what does a visitor use to decide what to believe about an object?**We look for reasons, such as a visible detail, a museum source or personal knowledge. We also ask when the person checks or rejects the answer.<br> | A connection to an object can come from its history, appearance or a personal memory. These are different reasons. We keep them separate when reading the visitor’s account. This uses relational authenticity and the difference between looking alike and having a real source link. We examine how the two people notice and deal with that difference. They can retain different readings.<br> | Authentication means a process through which an account comes to be accepted. We ask who accepts a museum explanation and on what grounds. Research on AI writing also helps us check whether a person still recognises their voice.
