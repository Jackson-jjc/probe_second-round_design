# Why five probes became two

## Five starting points

Ask, make, change, co-write and share. The earlier atlas records the initial five designs.

## Meeting · 2 Oct 2026

Make the activities easy to join. Give AI a clear role. Explore clothing and multilingual exchange. Probe 2 focuses on authenticity, while 4 and 5 can form one cross-cultural direction. The researcher confirmed this meeting date on 16 October.

## Probe interview · 9 Oct 2026

A researcher with a software-engineering background suggested a take-away card, choice about AI use and combining 4 with 5. They questioned the purpose of 1 and 3 and asked how two locations could use the same object.

## Two directions · 14–16 Oct 2026

Develop Probe 2 as A and combine 4 and 5 as B. Review the literature and the book cases to explain the design choices.

## Shared local interaction · 16 Oct 2026

Use a sleeve cuff or bag corner at one small capture station. A follows physical action and AI response. B develops one postcard through description, a real reply and clarification. The final card carries that exchange forward.

## Round 1 plan · 21–22 Oct 2026

Prepare low-fidelity prototypes of A and B for ERGO 117392. Use prepared AI examples, manual simulation, anonymous feedback and a local whiteboard. The protocol specifies this prototype round. A and B remain the working probe concepts.

## Clarify the design levels · 22 Oct 2026

Present the five-to-two selection, the two concepts and their first prototype round separately. Use Round 1 feedback to improve the directions, develop further iterations and later choose which direction to pursue.

## Full concepts and first prototypes · 23 Oct 2026

Clarify A’s arrangement, photograph and AI response. B can use digital exchange or a physical board with AI assistance, with printing as a later option. Map each activity to Round 1 while retaining the submitted protocol.

## Selection criterion

Reduce barriers of knowledge, language, manual skill, arriving alone and willingness to use AI. Prototype feedback helps identify useful participation improvements.

Keep 1 as an introduction. Develop 2 as A. Pause 3. Merge 4 and 5 as B.
