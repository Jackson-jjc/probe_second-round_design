# Numeric references

Search cut-off 14 October 2026. Original passages and figures checked 16 October 2026. Focused evidence synthesis, not a systematic review.

1. Jones, S. (2010). Negotiating Authentic Objects and Authentic Selves: Beyond the Deconstruction of Authenticity. Journal of Material Culture. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1177/1359183510364074)

   Reading scope: Classical theory. Relevant abstract and discussion passages were checked, rather than claiming a new full reading.

2. Grayson, K., & Martinec, R. (2004). Consumer Perceptions of Iconicity and Indexicality and Their Influence on Assessments of Authentic Market Offerings. Journal of Consumer Research. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1086/422109)

   Reading scope: Classical theory carried forward from the existing review and concept checks.

3. Hwang, A. H.-C., et al. (2025). “It was 80% me, 20% AI”: Seeking Authenticity in Co-Writing with Large Language Models. PACM HCI, 9(2), CSCW122. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711020)

   Reading scope: CSCW journal paper. Abstract, research questions and the existing review informed this synthesis.

4. He, Z., Su, J., Chen, L., Wang, T., & LC, R. (2025). “I Recall the Past”: Exploring How People Collaborate with Generative AI to Create Cultural Heritage Narratives. PACM HCI, 9(2), CSCW108. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1145/3711006)

   Reading scope: CSCW journal paper. Abstract and selected findings on familiar and unfamiliar heritage.

5. Vu, T. P., & Lee, B. (2026). TwistLens: A Docent-Informed Image Transformation to Create Previews That Prompt Anticipation and Interpretive Experiences Before Museum Visits. CHI 2026. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790352)

   Reading scope: CHI full paper. Author project and manuscript, used for its specific pre-visit task context.

6. Yuan, X., et al. (2026). Toward a culturally situated perspective: Rethinking the role of AI in intangible cultural heritage pattern creation. DRS 2026. [Source](https://doi.org/10.21606/drs.2026.2791)

   Reading scope: DRS design research paper. Selected methods, making reflections and cultural-judgment passages.

7. Chen, B., Li, Y., Wang, K., Zhang, W., Je, S., & Li, X. (2026). ZOMA: Integrating Gen-AI in hybrid crafts to promote cultural heritage transmission. International Journal of Human-Computer Studies, 215, 103856. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhcs.2026.103856)

   Reading scope: Abstract-level reading only. Details of participant background and interaction burden remain to be checked in the full text.

8. Agarwal, D., Naaman, M., & Vashistha, A. (2025). AI Suggestions Homogenize Writing Toward Western Styles and Diminish Cultural Nuances. CHI 2025. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713564)

   Reading scope: CHI full paper. Research context, abstract and selected results and discussion.

9. Xiao, Y., et al. (2025). Sustaining Human Agency, Attending to Its Cost: An Investigation into Generative AI Design for Non-Native Speakers’ Language Use. CHI 2025. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713626)

   Reading scope: CHI full paper. Abstract, interface Figure 2, discussion and limitations. The setting was US everyday information exchange.

10. Mylonas, D., Ahmed, R., Sinkeviciute, A., & Koliousis, A. (2026). Colour in Translation: Data, Models, and Benchmarking for Cross-Linguistic Colour Naming. CHI 2026. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791626)

   Reading scope: CHI full paper. Abstract and relevant colour-translation benchmark passages. No Chinese sample.

11. Li, H., Zhang, D., Chen, S., & Bryan-Kinns, N. (2026). The In-situ AI Pattern Merchant: A Speculative Intervention in Huayao Embroidery Futures. DRS 2026. [Source](https://doi.org/10.21606/drs.2026.1403)

   Reading scope: DRS paper. Selected intervention and interaction sections, including 4.2.3, and original Figures 4 and 12. Formal DOI 1403 replaces the old DOI on the author-version cover.

12. Bryan-Kinns, N., et al. (2026). Preliminary Explorations of Generative AI and the Embodied Embroidery of Huayao Intangible Cultural Heritage. CHI EA 2026. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772363.3798663)

   Reading scope: CHI extended abstract, not a CHI full paper. Making methods, results and discussion, with original Figure 5.

13. Kist, C., & Moshfeghi, Y. (2026). To Bot or Not? Conversational Agents and Authentic Heritage Experiences. CHI EA 2026. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772363.3798924)

   Reading scope: CHI extended abstract. A UK survey of 200 people, not deployment effects.

14. Han, L., et al. (2026). Gen-Diaolou: An Integrated AI-Assisted Interactive System for Diachronic Understanding and Preservation of the Kaiping Diaolou. CHI 2026. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790720)
