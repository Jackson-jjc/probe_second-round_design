# Direction B · One Object, Two Interpretations

Combines Probes 4 and 5. 6–10 minutes per turn, plus an optional postcard is an estimate.

Start a postcard on a cuff or bag corner, exchange it with a real visitor across languages, then clarify and keep the version that grows from the conversation.

## Research question

How do visitors retain and negotiate their understanding of a shared heritage object in AI-assisted cross-language exchanges?

The postcard is the shared object of the conversation from the first draft. One person expresses a connection between a heritage detail and a personal item. A real recipient responds to that draft. The sender clarifies and updates the same card, which becomes the take-away.

## Visitor activity

1. **Choose the source and local item.** Use the same named work, numbered detail and fact card. Choose a cuff or bag corner and approve one local crop C in the shared capture window. A prepared item or outline is available.

2. **Say what you want to express.** In your own language, say one short sentence linking the source detail with the item or an association. Check the words before AI uses them.

3. **Build and share draft P1.** AI translates the sentence and proposes a pattern preview for C. A fixed layout keeps the source, original words, translation and AI preview together. Approve what the real recipient may see.

4. **Receive a real reply.** The recipient sees P1 and the shared source. They point to a detail, explain their understanding or ask a question. The reply can come from an available partner or a consented earlier exchange.

5. **Clarify the same postcard.** Accept the reply, clarify the meaning or keep different readings. Update the words on the same card and request one revised image if useful. Keep P1, the reply and the final version P2 linked.

6. **Keep the conversation’s result.** Preview P2 with the chosen words, source credit and AI label. Discuss the future display rule, then choose download or optional one-off email. A card awaiting a real reply is labelled Draft awaiting reply.

## AI’s role

A language and image assistant around one developing postcard. It translates the visitor’s checked sentence and proposes a pattern adaptation on the approved cuff or bag crop. After the real reply, it can help express a clarification and update that same preview. Each person’s voice stays identified.

## The non-AI route

Use direct language skills or study-team translation and a plain postcard showing the source, local-item reference and checked words. The real reply and clarification still develop that same card. Choose translation, image generation and delivery separately.

## Smallest working set-up

Use the same fixed capture station as A. Add a short-message interface, a real reply channel or consented reply pool and one postcard template. Start with one draft image and one optional revision. A private delivery service can send the final card in the study deployment.

## Broad participation

Allow speech, short text, pointing, a prepared item and study-team language support. Visitors can arrive alone and receive a reply later. Keep the same card ID across turns. Plain cards, downloads and finishing without email are available choices.

## Candidate contribution

The candidate contribution is an account of how a source-related personal meaning develops through a multilingual conversation around one AI-assisted visual postcard. Its draft and revision show how people recognise, question and clarify each other’s interpretations. The final card keeps the outcome of that same process.

## Revision criterion

Check that a recipient replies to the actual source-linked draft. Follow whether the sender’s clarification changes the words, image or their stated connection to the work. Keep pending replies visible as pending. Test the full draft–reply–clarification–receipt journey.

### A real person reads my draft

Approve what the recipient may see. They receive the source detail, words, translation and local-item preview together.

Recipient: identifies a feature, explains their reading or asks a question. Their reply supplies evidence of what the draft conveyed. [8] [9]

### The reply shapes P2

Confirm, clarify or keep a different reading. AI can translate the clarification. Request one revised pattern preview if useful.

Record: keep P1, the actual reply, the clarification and final P2 under one card ID. Each word or image change has a place in the conversation.
