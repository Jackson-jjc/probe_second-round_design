One postcard carries the whole conversation

The postcard begins as a draft another visitor can respond to. Link a source detail with your cuff or bag corner and say what you want to express. AI helps picture and translate the idea. A real recipient replies to the draft. Clarify that same card, then keep the version that grows from the conversation.

BEGIN THE CARD

Source + local item + my meaning

Use the same capture window as A. Choose a cuff, bag corner or prepared study item. Say a sentence in your own language, such as “I want this line at my bag’s edge because it reminds me of a path.” Check the words.

AI: translates that sentence and proposes a local pattern preview. A fixed layout adds source credit, exact words and identified translation.

SHARE P1

A real person reads my draft

Approve what the recipient may see. They receive the source detail, words, translation and local-item preview together.

Recipient: identifies a feature, explains their reading or asks a question. Their reply supplies evidence of what the draft conveyed. [8] [9]

CLARIFY THE SAME CARD

The reply shapes P2

Confirm, clarify or keep a different reading. AI can translate the clarification. Request one revised pattern preview if useful.

Record: keep P1, the actual reply, the clarification and final P2 under one card ID. Each word or image change has a place in the conversation.

KEEP ITS RESULT

The final card is the take-away

Select the final words and image. An agreed excerpt of the real reply can appear with its contributor identified. Preview P2 and choose download or optional one-off email.

A card awaiting a real reply keeps that status visible.

What stays on the developing card

Four clear parts: unchanged source thumbnail and credit, local-item pattern preview, checked original words with identified translation, and draft or final status. The final card can retain an agreed reply excerpt. A fixed layout preserves words and credits as the image changes.

A personal association remains labelled as the visitor’s response. “A path” may be what a repeated line means to that person. The source’s documented background stays alongside it. A plain card carries the same source, local-item reference and words through the real exchange.

GIFT and museum data souvenirs provide precedents for object-related personal messages and take-aways. B brings the take-away into the conversation from its first draft. The card people keep is also the object through which they understood one another. [15] [26]
